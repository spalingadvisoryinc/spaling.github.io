Buyer FAQ

Bid with fewer surprises.

The essentials buyers usually need before registering, bidding and paying.

How do I register to bid?

Open the Premier Auctions bidding platform, create an account, and register for the auction you want to participate in. Registration is completed through the online bidding platform.

How does online bidding work?

You can place the next available bid or enter a maximum bid and allow the system to bid automatically on your behalf up to that amount. Timed auctions may use bid extensions when bidding occurs near closing.

How do I pay after winning?

Successful bidders receive an invoice after the sale. Premier currently lists e-transfer, cash, bank draft, certified corporate cheque, credit card up to the stated limit, and wire transfer as payment options. Auction-specific terms always apply.

Does Premier provide shipping?

Premier does not currently provide in-house shipping. Buyers should review the removal terms for the specific auction and arrange transportation as needed.

What is a buyer's premium?

A buyer's premium is an additional percentage added to the winning bid. The amount and applicable terms should be reviewed before bidding on each auction.

Where are Premier Auctions sales located?

Premier's headquarters is in Strathmore, Alberta, with yard locations in Strathmore and Alix. Auction assets may be located elsewhere across Western Canada, and each sale identifies where the items can be viewed and collected.
